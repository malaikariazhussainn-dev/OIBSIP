Biography Of Imran Khan


Imran Khan was born in Lahore, Pakistan, in 1952. He became one of Pakistan's most prominent international cricketers, making his Test debut in 1971 and later becoming captain of the national side.

His cricket career reached its defining moment in 1992, when he captained Pakistan to victory in the Cricket World Cup. The tournament became the final chapter of his international playing career and established a lasting part of his public legacy.

After cricket, Khan devoted significant attention to philanthropic and educational initiatives. Shaukat Khanum Memorial Cancer Hospital in Lahore opened in 1994, while Namal was established in Mianwali in 2008 as an educational initiative.

In politics, Khan founded Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf in 1996. He later became Prime Minister of Pakistan in August 2018 and remained in office until April 2022, when he lost a parliamentary vote of no confidence.
